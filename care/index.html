Privacy &
customer care.

How your details are used, how orders work, and where to get help.

Your privacy

Angel’s Haven receives the information you choose to submit. Our delivery form saves your name, WhatsApp number, order details, address, requested delivery date and instructions so we can arrange and confirm your delivery. The pop-up signup saves your email address, signup date and consent for event updates.

These forms store information in our hosted website database. Bakery orders submitted through Google Forms are handled by Google; messages sent through WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp. Those services have their own privacy policies. Please do not include card details, passwords or sensitive personal information in our forms.

Your treat list is stored in this browser tab’s session storage to carry it between pages. It is not a confirmed order. Google Fonts supplies the fonts used on this site, and hosting providers may process connection information to serve and protect the website. Form protection uses temporary rotating network-address digests to limit repeated submissions.

To request access to, correction or deletion of information you submitted, or to stop pop-up emails, contact Angel’s Haven on WhatsApp. Say whether your request concerns a delivery, order or email signup. Do not send identification documents unless we have agreed a necessary verification method.

Orders, payment & recurring plans

Prices are in Jamaican dollars. Menu subtotals cover items with listed prices; delivery and unpriced items need confirmation. A saved delivery request or copied treat list does not reserve products or a delivery slot. We confirm availability, the final total and payment details on WhatsApp before payment.

Full payment (100%) is required when your order is confirmed, and no later than the day before delivery. We send your payment-provider link on WhatsApp. This website does not collect card details or process payments directly; available payment methods are shown by the provider.

Weekly, bi-weekly and monthly selections are requests for recurring bakery deliveries. This website does not automatically charge a card or start a paid subscription. To change or stop a recurring arrangement, message us before your next order is confirmed.

Cancellations & refunds

If you need to cancel, contact us on WhatsApp with your order reference. Cancellation timing is measured from your confirmed delivery time, in Jamaica time.

80% refund

Cancel more than 24 hours before your confirmed delivery time. We retain 20% of the amount paid.

50% refund

Cancel 24 hours or less before your confirmed delivery time. We retain 50% of the amount paid for preparation and labour.

These terms apply to customer cancellations. If your order is damaged, incorrect or otherwise unsatisfactory, contact us promptly and include a photo when helpful so we can review and arrange an appropriate resolution. Nothing in this policy limits your applicable consumer rights.

Ingredients & allergies

Please ask about ingredients and allergens before ordering, and wait for our confirmation that we can accommodate your needs. Do not assume that a product is free from an allergen based on its name or photograph. Sample and digitally enhanced images are labeled on the menu; appearance may vary.

Need help?

For an order question, privacy request, unsubscribe request or website problem, message +1 (876) 212-0247. For a website issue, include the page name and what happened. Never send payment-card details, passwords or one-time codes.
