A NOTE FROM THE NEIGHBOURHOOD

A little about Caymanas Park

Located in Gregory Park, Portmore, St. Catherine, Caymanas Park is a long-standing home of Jamaican horse racing. It officially opened in August 1959.

For our pop-up story, the racing atmosphere is the backdrop to an easygoing day with something sweet in hand.
