A FEW SWEET DETAILS

Before you pre-order

How far ahead should I order?

Please allow at least three days for cookie pre-orders. Ask us on WhatsApp about lead times for other products and special requests.

Does a recurring plan charge me automatically?

No. Weekly, bi-weekly and monthly plans are arranged with us. We confirm dates, availability and payment details with you.

Can I request a change?

Message us as soon as possible. We’ll let you know what changes are possible before your order is prepared.
